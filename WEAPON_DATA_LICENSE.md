The bundled `js/weapon-catalog.js` contains the 38 standard weapons in the SRD 5.2.1 Weapons table (pages 91–92), including damage, properties, mastery, weight, and cost. Source: https://media.dndbeyond.com/compendium-images/srd/5.2/SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf. Weapon proficiency and mastery progression references were adapted from the user-supplied Character Keeper project with its author's express permission. The app bundles this data and does not fetch weapon data while playing.

Changes: the table was converted to structured JavaScript fields; weapon properties and mastery effects are summarized for the interface. Character-owned weapons can be customized with names, statistics, ability/proficiency overrides, quantities, ammunition, and limited uses. Mastery effects are presented as reminders; the app does not automatically resolve every target-dependent mastery effect. No paid weapon catalogue or magic-item collection is included.

This notice applies to included SRD material, not application code.
