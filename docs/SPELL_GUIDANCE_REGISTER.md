# The Folio · 2024 SRD Spell Guidance Register

Milestone 16.1 · 339 of 339 bundled spells covered.

This is a spell-by-spell editorial register of **original tactical suggestions**, not a replacement for the precise SRD rules in `js/spell-catalog.js`. Those bundled descriptions, casting times, components, durations, saving throws, damage, ritual properties, and upcasting text remain the mechanical reference displayed inside the app. Guidance records are stored in `js/spell-audit.js`. Every row was mapped by an exact catalogue ID and checked for coverage and missing metadata.

**Scope/limits:** The spell dataset is the 2024 Open5e SRD snapshot fetched November 11, 2026 (`SPELL_DATA_LICENSE.md`). We also reviewed a selection of high-impact 2024 differences and tested them. This work is **not** a 339-entry independent, paragraph-by-paragraph verification against every official SRD 5.2.1 erratum. Before publicly claiming full rules certification or importing a new dataset, compare source descriptions against the latest official SRD: https://www.dndbeyond.com/srd. Character-specific spell access, multiclass rules, and expansions outside the SRD are separate milestones.
