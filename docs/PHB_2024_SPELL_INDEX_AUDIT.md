# 2024 Player’s Handbook — spell-index reconciliation (Milestone 17.5)

**Coverage:** 339 bundled SRD spell descriptions + **52 additional PHB-only names** = **391 unique indexed spells**. This is a *name, level and class-list index*, not 391 full rules implementations. Reference-only spells do not include their proprietary mechanics.

Entries below are only identifying metadata, not excerpts from sourcebook spell descriptions. For official wording, consult your copy of the PHB or the official online spell listing. This register is not a complete index of spells in every official sourcebook.
