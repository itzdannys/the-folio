# Rules, data, and code provenance

This work includes material from the System Reference Document 5.2.1 ("SRD 5.2.1") by Wizards of the Coast LLC, available at https://www.dndbeyond.com/srd. The SRD 5.2.1 is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License, available at https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode.

The builder includes class/Hit Die/saving throw summary information, class progression, permitted species and backgrounds, and selected subclass/feat names used to support the player's offline digital character sheets. Rule descriptions have been condensed or written anew, and complex traits or subclass abilities have not been reproduced wholesale.

The full-caster, half-caster, third-caster, and Pact Magic slot progressions and the class spell-preparation tables in `js/builder.js` and `js/rules-engine.js` were adapted, with the original author's express permission, from the user-supplied **Character Keeper** project. Weapon catalogue and weapon-rule data were also adapted from that project with permission. The spell and weapon data snapshots are separately attributed in `SPELL_DATA_LICENSE.md`, `WEAPON_DATA_LICENSE.md`, and `EQUIPMENT_DATA_LICENSE.md`.

No proprietary player handbook scans, user-owned PDFs, or private game data are included in this project ZIP. This application is independent of Wizards of the Coast.

Milestone 17.5 includes the names and limited identifying metadata of 52 additional *Player’s Handbook (2024)* spells as **reference-only** entries; their proprietary descriptions and mechanics are **not** reproduced. Seventeen PHB naming variants are search aliases for existing SRD descriptions. The PHB is © Wizards of the Coast. The full-index comparison was checked against official PHB update information and publicly listed spell names; this is not an authorization to distribute the book's rules text. Personal imported entries stay on the user's device.

Milestone 17.6 adds **39 additional names and identifying metadata** from four proprietary official supplements (*Explorer’s Guide to Wildemount*, *Strixhaven: A Curriculum of Chaos*, *Tasha’s Cauldron of Everything*, and *Fizban’s Treasury of Dragons*). They are unlicensed reference-only index entries, **not** included commercial spell descriptions, effect text, or executable mechanics. Consult the sourcebook before using them. Names and references remain attributed to Wizards of the Coast LLC; details and provenance are listed in `docs/SUPPLEMENT_SPELL_INDEX_AUDIT.md`.
